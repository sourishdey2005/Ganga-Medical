# **App Name**: GangaRx

## Core Features:

- Intelligent Prescription Scanning: An AI tool that processes uploaded prescriptions via OCR to identify medicine names, dosages, and quantities for pharmacist verification.
- Dynamic Digital Pharmacy Catalog: A high-performance product browsing experience with categorized listings for prescription drugs, OTC medicines, and wellness devices.
- Prescription Management System: A robust workflow for users to upload medical documents and for pharmacists to review, adjust, and approve medicine requests.
- Patient Health Vault: Secure storage for all digital medical records including previous prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and payment invoices.
- Secure Transaction Hub: Full integration with Razorpay supporting UPI, credit/debit cards, and digital wallets for seamless medical procurement.
- Real-time Order Tracking: A lightweight interface providing visual updates on order preparation, packing, and dispatch progress.
- Direct WhatsApp Concierge: A persistent floating interface linking users to direct store assistance for immediate pharmaceutical queries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Clinical Azure (#297CA1) – a trust-inspiring, focused blue that suggests precision and medical expertise.
- Background Color: Glacial White (#F8FAFB) – a heavily desaturated blue tint providing a sterile, spacious, and calming aesthetic.
- Accent Color: Deep Emerald (#12866B) – a high-contrast analogous shade used for primary calls-to-action and critical alerts.
- Font: 'Inter' – selected for its objective, neutral, and machined look that ensures absolute legibility for medical dosage and labels.
- Medical-grade line icons with consistent 2px stroke weights for a crisp, professional, and accessible visual language.
- A high-fidelity minimalist grid layout with generous white space and shadow-defined card components to reduce cognitive load for patients.
- Linear micro-interactions for state transitions and subtle loading shimmers for AI processing phases to provide a premium, reactive feel.
